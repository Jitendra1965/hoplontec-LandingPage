NiviLog — How to use your food and feelings journal
English narration · Illustrative sample journal

00:00:00  WELCOME
Welcome to NiviLog, your private food and feelings journal. Here's how to make it part of your day.

00:00:07  01  /  YOUR JOURNAL
Start in Logs. Your meals and check-ins stay together, and the filters help you find earlier entries.

00:00:15  02  /  ADD A MEAL
To log a meal, tap Add an entry. Describe what you ate, including amounts when you can, then tap Review and save.

00:00:22  03  /  CHECK THE MATCHES
Check the foods NiviLog found. These are suggested matches, so correct anything that doesn't reflect your meal.

00:00:30  04  /  CONFIRM PORTIONS
Tap Edit to adjust a portion or choose another food. When the foods and amounts look right, save your meal.

00:00:37  05  /  DAILY VIEW
Daily View brings your logged nutrition together. These are estimates, and they only include the meals and portions you've recorded.

00:00:45  06  /  YOUR GOALS
In Settings, open Nutrition goals to choose daily markers. Set targets that suit your plan; the app's starting values aren't personal recommendations.

00:00:55  07  /  HOW YOU FEEL
For a check-in, choose How I'm feeling. Record a feeling, mood, or energy rating, then save. Add notes whenever they're useful.

00:01:03  08  /  LOOK BACK
In Patterns, look back over seven, thirty, or ninety days. Days without entries stay blank, so gaps aren't mistaken for low ratings.

00:01:12  09  /  NUTRITION TRENDS
Switch to Nutrition to follow protein, carbs, fat, and fiber. Tap the chart to explore a day's logged values.

00:01:20  10  /  FOOD & FEELINGS
Food and feelings can highlight possible associations when enough records are available. These observations don't prove a cause or diagnose an allergy.

00:01:30  11  /  MAKE IT YOURS
Choose English or Spanish in Settings. Adjust text size, contrast, and other accessibility options to make your journal comfortable to use.

00:01:39  YOUR NEXT STEP
NiviLog works offline and is free to use. Start with a meal and one check-in. A product by Hoplontec.
