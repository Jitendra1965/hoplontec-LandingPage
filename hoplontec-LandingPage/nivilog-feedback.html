How your feedback is handled

We send only your rating, chosen topic, comment, form language and a random submission reference. No app account, journal, photos or device identifier is attached. GitHub hosts this page; FormSubmit processes the submission and emails it to Hoplontec. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address. FormSubmit may present a CAPTCHA. This page has no analytics or advertising scripts.

FormSubmit documents a 30-day submission archive. Hoplontec keeps product feedback for up to 180 days to review and prioritize improvements. This form cannot provide a personal reply. Submitting feedback is optional and does not affect your access to NiviLog.
