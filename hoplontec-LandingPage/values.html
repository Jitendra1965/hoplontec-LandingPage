OUR COMPANY / OUR VALUES

What matters.
How we build.

Respect for people. Strength in collaboration. Clarity in how we think and communicate.

01

Respect

Treat people with dignity. Listen to different perspectives, value each person’s contribution, and respect the communities we serve.

02

Collaboration

Work together, share knowledge, and help each other succeed. Bring different perspectives together to solve problems.

03

Think straight. Talk straight.

Think clearly, question assumptions, and speak honestly. Say what we mean with care and respect, and make room for constructive disagreement.

04

Simplicity

Make powerful tools approachable. Focus on what matters and remove unnecessary complexity.

05

Purposeful innovation

Use advanced technology to solve real problems. Let the needs of the people we serve guide what we build.

06

Accountability

Take ownership of our decisions and commitments. Acknowledge mistakes, learn from them, and follow through.
