A JOURNAL THAT CONNECTS THE DETAILS

Meals, check-ins, and the bigger picture.

Designed for adults who want a clearer record of what they eat and how they feel, without losing the details of everyday life.

Capture your meals

Write a meal description, review the suggested foods and portions, then save. Edit entries, reuse recent meals, and find past records with search and filters.

See your day together

Bring meal records and estimated nutrition into a daily view. Look at energy, macronutrients, and the foods that contribute to your totals.

Record how you feel

Add optional check-ins for feelings, mood, physical energy, and symptoms. Look back over 7, 30, or 90 days.

Explore your patterns

With your permission, explore associations between recorded foods, nutrients, and your check-ins. Missing records stay unknown rather than being filled in.

YOU REVIEW. YOU DECIDE.

A useful starting point.
Your final say.

Meal-photo assistance and supported on-device tools can help prepare an entry. You check the food matches and portions before saving them to your journal.

Nutrition estimates use bundled food references and the portions you confirm. Optional on-device AI features depend on your device and model availability.

  1. CaptureDescribe your meal or use the optional photo assistance.
  2. ReviewCheck foods, adjust portions, and correct the details.
  3. ReflectReturn to your daily view and optional check-ins.

PRIVACY & CHOICE

Your journal, with controls that matter.

Local by default

The development build keeps journals encrypted on your device and uses device authentication to unlock them.

Optional connections

Pattern analysis, voice, and Google Sheets sync are separate choices. Google Sheets sync is optional; synced Sheets and exports are readable copies outside local encryption.

Access to your records

Local export, local erasure, and consent controls are part of the app. Connected Google copies are managed separately.

A journal for reflection.

NiviLog is not a diagnostic tool. Nutrition values are estimates, and patterns in journal entries do not establish cause and effect, diagnose allergies, or identify safe foods or portions.
