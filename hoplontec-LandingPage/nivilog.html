Less to remember.
More to look back on.

What was in yesterday’s lunch? How was your energy this week? A personal record gives those everyday details a place to live.

NiviLog is designed for people who want to build a food journal, keep track of their own check-ins, or explore their records over time. Start with a meal. Add how you feel when you want to. Return to the bigger picture at your own pace.

THE STORY BEHIND NIVILOG

A friend’s idea.
A gift carried forward.

NiviLog began with an idea from a dear friend who was living with a serious illness. He wanted a simple way to record how he felt throughout the day—before and after meals, or after physical activity.

The first version was small and simple, built around that wish. As the app evolved, his idea remained at its heart: making space to notice, remember, and reflect on everyday wellbeing.

My friend passed away in February 2026, one day before his birthday. NiviLog carries his idea forward.

“NiviLog is my small gift from my friend to anyone who takes a thoughtful interest in their own health.”

A personal note from NiviLog’s creator

A JOURNAL THAT CONNECTS THE DETAILS

Meals, check-ins, and the bigger picture.

Designed for adults who want a clearer record of what they eat and how they feel, without losing the details of everyday life.

Capture your meals

Write a meal description, review the suggested foods and portions, then save. Edit entries, reuse recent meals, and find past records with search and filters.

See your day together

Bring meal records and estimated nutrition into a daily view. Look at energy, macronutrients, and the foods that contribute to your totals.

Record how you feel

Add optional check-ins for feelings, mood, physical energy, and symptoms. Look back over 7, 30, or 90 days.

Explore your patterns

With your permission, explore associations between recorded foods, nutrients, and your check-ins. Missing records stay unknown rather than being filled in.

A LOOK INSIDE NIVILOG

From a small note to a useful record.

A simple rhythm for everyday journaling: capture the moment, check the details, and come back when you want to reflect.

Start with your own words

Describe what you ate, repeat a recent meal, or try photo assistance. Add the date and time so your record fits your day, even when you log it later.

Make the details yours

Check suggested foods and portions before saving. Correct a match or change an amount; nutrition estimates follow the details you confirm.

Look back with context

Review feelings, mood, energy, or symptoms over 7, 30, or 90 days. See how many days you recorded, with gaps left visible rather than guessed.

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Before you begin.

More about how NiviLog is being built, and what to expect at launch.

When can I download NiviLog?

NiviLog is coming soon. App Store and Google Play links will be added here when available. A release date and pricing have not been announced.

Who is NiviLog for?

NiviLog is for adults aged 18 and over who want a personal record of meals and wellbeing check-ins. You can keep a food journal, review nutrition estimates, or look back on how you have been feeling.

Can I use it offline?

The app is designed to keep core journaling, nutrition calculations, photo assistance, and pattern views on your device. Google Sheets sync requires a connection. Voice recognition may use your device provider’s online service, and optional AI models may need a download first.

Do I need AI to log a meal?

No. You can type a description and review foods and portions without AI. Optional on-device AI assistance depends on compatible Apple Intelligence or Gemini Nano devices and model availability. Photo assistance is a separate offline feature, and suggested matches still need your review.

What happens to my meal photos?

In the development app, meal photos are processed locally to suggest foods. They are not uploaded or saved to the journal. You review the suggestions and save the food details you choose.

Can patterns explain what is causing a symptom?

No. Journal patterns describe associations in the entries you record; they do not establish a cause, diagnose a condition, or identify safe foods. NiviLog is a reflection tool, not a substitute for medical care.

Can I export or delete my journal?

The app includes local export and erasure controls, protected by device authentication. If you choose Google Sheets sync or make an export, those copies exist separately from the encrypted local journal and must be managed separately.

A journal for reflection.

NiviLog is not a diagnostic tool. Nutrition values are estimates, and patterns in journal entries do not establish cause and effect, diagnose allergies, or identify safe foods or portions.
